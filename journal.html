Journal of Epistemic Research Frameworks (JERF)

The Journal of Epistemic Research Frameworks (JERF) is an open-access scholarly journal associated with the Epistemic Research Frameworks (ERF) Institute.

Editorial Mission

The journal publishes scholarly work devoted to conceptual, methodological, and foundational inquiry across disciplines.
