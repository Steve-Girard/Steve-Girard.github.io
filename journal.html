Journal of Epistemic Research Frameworks (JERF)

The Journal of Epistemic Research Frameworks (JERF) is an open-access scholarly journal associated with the Epistemic Research Frameworks (ERF) Institute. The journal focuses on conceptual, methodological, and foundational research across disciplines, with particular attention to how scientific knowledge is produced, interpreted, and evaluated.

JERF publishes work on scientific reasoning, conceptual frameworks, historical and reconstructive inference, theoretical modelling, and related questions about the foundations of knowledge. The journal welcomes rigorous contributions that clarify, refine, and strengthen scholarly inquiry.

Submission Guidelines

JERF welcomes original contributions addressing conceptual, methodological, and foundational questions across disciplines. The journal encourages work that combines intellectual rigor, conceptual clarity, careful reasoning, and meaningful engagement with relevant evidence, methods, or theoretical frameworks.

Contributions are evaluated according to their scholarly merit rather than disciplinary fashion, publication trends, or institutional affiliation. Particular value is placed on methodological transparency, conceptual refinement, constructive analysis, and work that advances understanding.

Authors are encouraged to write with precision, respect for evidence, and awareness of alternative interpretations. JERF welcomes thoughtful scholarship that seeks to clarify questions, strengthen inquiry, and contribute responsibly to ongoing intellectual discussion.

Peer Review Process

All submissions undergo an initial editorial evaluation to determine their relevance to the journal's scope and standards of scholarship. Manuscripts that do not meet these basic criteria may be declined without external review.

Submissions that pass the initial evaluation are normally reviewed by independent scholars with relevant expertise. Reviews focus on conceptual coherence, methodological rigor, evidential accountability, clarity of argumentation, and engagement with existing scholarship.

Acceptance is not determined by agreement with an author's conclusions, disciplinary affiliation, institutional prestige, or theoretical orientation. The central question is whether a submission makes a clear, rigorous, and intellectually responsible contribution to scholarly inquiry.

The Editorial Office may request revisions, seek additional evaluations, or decline submissions when significant concerns remain unresolved.

Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance is based on the quality of the contribution rather than its disciplinary affiliation, institutional origin, or theoretical orientation. JERF evaluates submissions according to standards of intellectual rigor, conceptual clarity, methodological transparency, and evidential accountability.

Manuscripts should present a clearly identifiable scholarly contribution, demonstrate familiarity with relevant scholarship, and engage competing interpretations fairly and responsibly.

Particular value is placed on originality of insight, coherence of analysis, reproducibility of reasoning, and the capacity to clarify, refine, or strengthen existing frameworks of inquiry.

Contributions are expected to be supported by appropriate evidence, reasoning, or methodological justification. Work that relies primarily on rhetoric, unsupported speculation, ideological advocacy, or avoidable ambiguity is unlikely to satisfy the journal's standards for publication.

Copyright Policy

Authors retain copyright of their work. By publishing in JERF, they grant the journal a non-exclusive right to publish, distribute, archive, and preserve the accepted version of the manuscript.

Authors remain free to share, reproduce, and republish their work elsewhere, provided that the original publication in the Journal of Epistemic Research Frameworks (JERF) is appropriately acknowledged.

Responsibility for published content remains with the authors. The views expressed in published contributions do not necessarily reflect those of the Editorial Team or the Epistemic Research Frameworks (ERF) Institute.

Publication Timelines

JERF seeks to provide timely editorial decisions while maintaining rigorous scholarly standards. Review times may vary depending on the complexity of a submission and the availability of qualified reviewers.

Authors will be informed of significant developments throughout the review process. The journal values transparency and strives to maintain clear communication at each stage of evaluation.

The journal seeks to balance efficiency with the deliberative standards expected of serious scholarly inquiry.

As an annual publication, JERF prioritizes careful review, constructive feedback, and editorial consistency. The journal believes that thoughtful evaluation ultimately serves both authors and readers alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions below address common issues related to submission, evaluation, and scholarly contribution.

May authors submit work from any discipline?

Yes. JERF evaluates submissions according to scholarly quality rather than disciplinary origin.

Are interdisciplinary submissions encouraged?

Yes. Interdisciplinary contributions are welcomed when they strengthen conceptual clarity, methodological understanding, or coherent frameworks of inquiry.

May authors challenge established theories or prevailing interpretations?

Yes. Critical examination of existing frameworks is encouraged when supported by clear reasoning, appropriate evidence, and responsible scholarly engagement.

Does JERF publish negative results or unresolved questions?

Yes. Such contributions may be valuable when presented with rigor and scholarly discipline.

What distinguishes a publishable article from an interesting idea?

An interesting idea becomes a publishable contribution when it is developed through explicit reasoning, methodological transparency, engagement with relevant scholarship, and a clear demonstration of scholarly significance.

What is the journal's position on speculative work?

Speculative work may be considered when assumptions are explicit and their relation to existing evidence or theory is responsibly addressed.

Call for Contributions

JERF welcomes contributions from scholars whose work engages conceptual, methodological, and foundational questions relevant to the production, interpretation, and evaluation of knowledge. The journal is particularly interested in manuscripts that clarify, challenge, refine, or strengthen existing frameworks of inquiry through rigorous reasoning and methodological transparency.

Authors from all disciplines, institutional affiliations, and scholarly traditions are encouraged to contribute. Particular value is placed on conceptual precision, evidential accountability, intellectual discipline, and a willingness to engage difficult questions with scholarly seriousness.

Editorial Perspective

The Journal of Epistemic Research Frameworks is founded on a simple premise: the quality of knowledge depends not only on the results that inquiry produces, but also on the conceptual and methodological conditions that make those results meaningful. JERF exists to support careful scholarship devoted to the examination, clarification, and refinement of those conditions.

The journal values intellectual rigor over immediacy, conceptual precision over novelty alone, and disciplined reasoning over rhetorical persuasion. Its purpose is not to maximize publication volume, but to provide a stable venue for contributions that strengthen the foundations of scholarly inquiry and encourage thoughtful engagement across disciplinary boundaries.
