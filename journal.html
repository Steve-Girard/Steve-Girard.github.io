The journal project initially presented as the Journal of Epistemic Research Frameworks (JERF) is now being developed under the title Canadian Journal of Knowledge and Inquiry.

The journal is published by Epistemic Research Frameworks (ERF). Its first issue is in preparation.

The journal’s current presentation, editorial policies, peer review procedures, and author guidelines are available on its new website.
